# Ch. 5 — The Brainwave Bands, Honestly
*Part II — How It Actually Works · Target: 9 pp · Write in Week 1 (Jul 6–12) · Status: not started · ⚠ FACT-CHECK GATE: every research claim cited or softened before launch*

## Purpose & role in the argument
The honest science chapter — where the book earns the word "honest" in its subtitle. Give the reader the standard band taxonomy, then draw the line between what entrainment research actually supports and what marketing claims. This chapter carries the highest overclaim risk in the book; its citations feed Appendix C directly.

## Key points to hit
- The five bands, plainly: delta (0.5–3 Hz, deep sleep), theta (4–7 Hz), alpha (8–12 Hz), beta (13–30 Hz), gamma (30+ Hz) — what EEG in each band correlates with, stated as correlation
- What entrainment research supports vs. doesn't: modest, real effects in some studies (relaxation, attention, sleep onset); small samples, inconsistent replication, strong placebo components — report the limitations as carefully as the findings
- The marketing leap: "theta is the manifestation frequency" — how a correlational EEG label becomes a causal product claim; anatomy of that rhetorical move so the reader can spot it anywhere
- How to read any frequency claim critically: What's the mechanism? What's the measured outcome? Who measured it? Would this survive the Ch. 2 test?
- Worked example of band mislabeling from the audit sample (below) — even when a track IS binaural, the band can be wrong
