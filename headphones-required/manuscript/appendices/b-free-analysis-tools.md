## Purpose & role
The tool-by-tool instruction companion to Ch. 2 (mono-sum) and Ch. 11 (full audit). Screenshots + numbered steps; a non-technical reader must be able to follow every procedure cold. This appendix also future-proofs the book: chapters carry the judgment, this appendix carries the (updatable) tool steps.

## Contents
- **Audacity** (free, all platforms — the workhorse):
  - Mono-sum test: import → mix stereo down to mono → listen for the beat's disappearance (Ch. 2's test, canonical steps live HERE, lead magnet and chapter both reference them)
  - Spectral peek: split stereo to two tracks → Analyze → Plot Spectrum per channel → read the carrier peaks → subtract for beat frequency
  - Phase-inversion layer isolation (Ch. 9 / Ch. 11): invert one channel and mix to expose/kill the difference content
- **Spek** (or equivalent free spectrogram viewer): whole-file spectrogram at a glance — spotting ramps (or their absence) visually over time
- **A LUFS meter** (free option, e.g. a loudness meter plugin or youlean free tier): checking session loudness against the Ch. 8 targets
- **The no-install versions:** phone/speaker quick mono test; any free online tone generator for hearing what a real binaural beat sounds like (a reference experience, so the reader knows what they're testing for)
- Per-tool: what it can tell you, what it can't, one screenshot per decisive step
