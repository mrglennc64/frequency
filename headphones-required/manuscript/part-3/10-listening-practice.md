# Ch. 10 — Listening Practice
*Part III — The Craft · Target: 5 pp · Write in Week 5 (Aug 3–9) · Status: not started*

## Purpose & role in the argument
The practical-use chapter — and the book's quiet thesis: the practice around the audio matters more than the audio. Deliberately deflates gear-lust and product-dependence, which paradoxically builds trust (the engineer telling you not to overspend).

## Key points to hit
- **Headphone selection:** what actually matters (stereo isolation, comfort for the session length, working L/R channels) and what doesn't (price, brand, "audiophile" tiers) — a €30 pair that seals well beats €400 open-backs for this purpose; include the 5-second L/R check
- **Volume:** comfortable-conversation level or below; louder is not stronger — entrainment is not a dose you can crank; sleep sessions especially (ties to Ch. 8 LUFS targets)
- **Environment & timing:** interruption-free beats acoustically perfect; matching band to time of day (beta sessions at bedtime work against you)
- **Consistency:** the practice variable with the strongest evidence behind it — a mediocre-but-daily practice beats a perfect-but-occasional one
- The reframe that sets up Part IV: the audio is a *state cue*, and cues work through repetition and ritual — which is why the behavioral wrapper (Ch. 13) is where results actually come from
