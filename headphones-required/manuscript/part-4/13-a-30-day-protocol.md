# Ch. 13 — A 30-Day Protocol
*Part IV — The Practice · Target: 7 pp · Write in Week 6 (Aug 10–16) · Status: not started*

## Purpose & role in the argument
The book's constructive payoff: an honest, structured 30-day practice that combines sessions with the behavioral work the gurus skip — plus a measurement journal so the reader generates their own evidence. This chapter is what makes the book a *practice guide* rather than only an exposé, and it's V9's script backbone.

## Key points to hit
- **Design principles first:** one intent per 30-day block (sleep, focus, or relaxation — not "abundance"); fixed time of day; session + behavior pairing (the part the gurus skip)
- **The protocol, week by week:**
  - Days 1–3: baseline journaling only, no audio (the control the genre never runs)
  - Days 4–24: daily session (band matched to intent per Ch. 5, listened per Ch. 10) immediately followed by its paired behavior — e.g. sleep intent → session + consistent lights-out; focus intent → session + one defined deep-work block
  - Days 25–27: audio-free days mid-practice (does the state cue survive without the cue?)
  - Days 28–30: review against baseline
- **The measurement journal** (included as a designed template, listed in PROGRESS as part of the design pass): 2 minutes/day — sleep latency estimate or focus-block count, 1–5 state rating, one line of notes; simple before/after comparison instructions, honest about noise and expectation effects
- Expectations calibration: what a win looks like (falling asleep 10 minutes faster is a *big* result), and permission to conclude "this does nothing for me" — the protocol respects a null result
- Session sourcing: any track that passes the Ch. 11 audit works; the €97 tier's 3 verified demonstration sessions are pre-cleared for exactly this protocol (one per intent: sleep / focus / relaxation)
