# Lead Magnet Spec — "The Test Almost Everything Fails"
*Standalone Ch. 2 PDF · Build in Week 3 (Jul 20–26) · Free, gated on email · Companion video: V1 (public YouTube) · Status: not started*

## Job
The funnel's front door, "designed to be shared and to sting." A reader who has never heard of Glenn Carter downloads it, runs the 10-minute mono-sum test on a track they own, watches it fail, and now (a) trusts the source that armed them and (b) wants the rest of the story. It must deliver full standalone value — the sting only spreads if the PDF is worth forwarding with no purchase.

## Format
- 8–12 pp PDF in the audit report-engine design system (waveform mark, indigo/purple palette, stat tiles, verdict-badge styling) — the design itself signals "this is the person who wrote that forensic report"
- Footer disclaimer on every page: *Educational audio-engineering analysis — not medical or therapeutic advice*
- Title on cover: **The Test Almost Everything Fails** · subtitle: *A 10-minute check for any "binaural" track you own* · byline: Glenn Carter · Frequency Engineering

## Contents (write once — this text back-ports into book Ch. 2 in Week 4)
1. **One-page setup:** the one rule most creators miss — the beat is generated in your brain from the difference between two carriers; no headphones (or no two carriers) = physically impossible. (Reuse audit methodology paragraph.)
2. **The test, two versions:**
   - Full version: Audacity mono-sum, numbered steps + screenshots (these steps are the master copy for Appendix B)
   - Quick version: no-install phone/speaker check
3. **Reading your result:** unchanged in mono = no binaural construction (what that means for what you bought); beat vanishes = real build (what still isn't proven — honest scope)
4. **One worked example of each outcome,** audit-styled with verdict badges (fail example modeled on the "528 Hz Abundance" mono file)
5. **The one-page checklist card** (printable)
6. **Last page — the two CTAs:** the book ("this test is Chapter 2 of 14 — the rest teaches you the whole craft") and, softer, the audit ("rather have your whole library done forensically?")

## Distribution
- Gated download behind email capture on the opt-in page (page copy = Week 3 deliverable, see `../launch/funnel-checklist.md`)
- V1 on YouTube is the ungated version of the same argument; its description links the PDF
- Explicitly licensed to forward/share intact — sharing IS the strategy

## Success criteria
A non-technical reader completes the test unaided in ≤10 minutes. Test this on one real person before shipping.
