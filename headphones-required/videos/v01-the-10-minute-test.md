**Thesis:** Most "binaural" tracks physically cannot work — and you can prove it yourself in ten minutes, free.

**Hook idea:** Play a bestselling-style "binaural" track, sum it to mono on screen, nothing changes. "That track cannot do what it sold you. Here's why — and how to test every track you own."

**Takeaway card:** "If it sounds the same in mono, there is no binaural beat. Headphones required — or it isn't binaural."
