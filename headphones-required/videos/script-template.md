# Video Script Template — Headphones Required companion series
*Applies to V1–V10 · 5–12 min each · Avatar/visual pipeline · The production quality IS part of the pitch (it demonstrates Signature Production)*

## Structure (timing blocks for a ~8-min target; scale proportionally)

### 1. HOOK — 0:00–0:30
One concrete, surprising claim or demonstration promise. Never a greeting, never "in this video I'll…". Best hooks in this series are measurements: *"This track sold as 6 Hz theta. I measured it. It's 11.2 Hz — a different brainwave band entirely."*

### 2. CLAIM — 0:30–1:30
State the video's single thesis in plain words, and what the viewer will be able to DO by the end (test something, spot something, build something). One thesis per video — cut anything that serves a second one.

### 3. DEMONSTRATION — 1:30 to ~70% of runtime
The core block. Show, don't assert: screen-share the spectrum, run the mono-sum live, play the before/after. Every video in this series must contain at least one **on-screen measurement or construction** — that's the format's signature and the differentiator from talking-head guru content.

### 4. TAKEAWAY — ~70–90%
The portable rule the viewer keeps: one sentence they could repeat to a friend. Restate it on-screen as a text card.

### 5. CTA — final 30–45 s
Per the video's brief. Public videos (V1): lead magnet → book. Paid-tier videos (V2–V10): the next video + the relevant funnel step (audit / Forge waitlist), soft register, never more than one CTA.

## Production notes (all videos)
- Avatar pipeline; audit design system for lower-thirds, text cards, and diagrams (indigo/purple palette, waveform mark, stat-tile style)
- Any audio played as "binaural" in a video must carry an on-screen note: *headphone demo — meaningless on speakers* (practice what the book preaches)
- Footer disclaimer on end card: "Educational audio-engineering analysis — not medical or therapeutic advice"
- Captions mandatory (much of the audience will preview muted)
- Script length guide: ~130 wpm spoken → 8 min ≈ 1,000–1,100 words
- Each script file: HOOK / CLAIM / DEMO / TAKEAWAY / CTA headings, with [SCREEN: …] directions inline
