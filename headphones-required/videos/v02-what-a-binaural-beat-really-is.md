# V2 — What a Binaural Beat Really Is
*€97 tier · ~8 min · Script Week 5, produce Week 5–6 · Source: Ch. 4 · Status: not started*

**Thesis:** The beat isn't in the file — it's made in your brain from two carriers, one per ear. Everything about correct frequency audio follows from that fact.

**Hook idea:** "There is no 10 Hz tone in this 10 Hz binaural track. Nothing in the file oscillates at 10 Hz. And that's exactly how it's supposed to work."

**Demo set-piece:** Build one live — generate 200 Hz left, 210 Hz right on screen; show each channel's spectrum (no 10 Hz anywhere); listener hears the 10 Hz beat on headphones. Then collapse to mono and lose it.

**Takeaway card:** "Two carriers. Two ears. One brain. The beat exists only in your head — which is why headphones are not a suggestion."
