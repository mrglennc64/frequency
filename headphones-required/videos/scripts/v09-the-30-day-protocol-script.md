Every manifestation channel tells you to listen every day. None of them tell you to spend the first three days listening to nothing. [pause] That empty window — the baseline — is the single most important part of this protocol, and it's the part that turns "it works for me" into actual evidence.

### CLAIM
The audio is a state tool. States pay off through behavior. So we're going to run a real experiment on you: one intent, a baseline to measure against, the session welded to a behavior, and a two-minute journal. Thirty days. At the end, you'll have something no testimonial can give you — your own numbers.

### DEMONSTRATION
[SCREEN: animated 30-day timeline]

Pick one intent — sleep, focus, or downshift. Not "abundance," that's not a state. Just one, for the month.

Days one to three — baseline. No audio. Just journal the thing you care about: how long to fall asleep, how many deep work blocks, your evening tension. Three days of "before."

Days four to twenty-four — the practice. Session at the same time every day, the paired behavior right after — for sleep, a fixed lights-out; for focus, one defined work block, started when the session starts. Two minutes of journal. And watch for the cue kicking in around week two — the descent starting earlier than it used to.

[SCREEN: days 25–27 highlighted, no audio]

Days twenty-five to twenty-seven — take the audio away again, but keep the time slot and the behavior. Does the state survive without the cue? If the practice built something, part of it is yours now, not the file's.

Days twenty-eight to thirty — bring it back, then review against your baseline.

### TAKEAWAY
[SCREEN: text card — "One intent. A baseline. A paired behavior."]

And read the result like an engineer. A win is modest and specific — falling asleep ten minutes faster is a big result. If the gains held through the audio-free days, you built a practice. If they vanished, the audio's doing real work — keep it. And if nothing moved at all? This protocol respects that answer too.
