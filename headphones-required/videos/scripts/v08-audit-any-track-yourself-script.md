### DEMONSTRATION
[SCREEN: check 1 — mono-sum + inversion]

Check one — binaural integrity. Mono-sum it. If nothing changes, there's no binaural construction, and if the label said binaural, the audit's basically over. Invert a channel too — if it cancels to silence, the two sides were identical. Fake stereo, proven.

[SCREEN: check 2 — carrier peaks, subtract]

Check two — frequency accuracy. Solo the left channel, read the carrier peak. Solo the right, read that one. Subtract. Does the beat land in the band on the label? This is where "six-hertz theta" turns out to be eleven-point-two.

[SCREEN: check 3 — spectrogram shape]

Check three — entrainment design. Open the whole file in a spectrogram and look at its shape over time. Is there a ramp in at the start? A steady plateau? A ramp out at the end? Or is it one flat, unchanging stripe — proof nobody made a single decision after the render button.

[SCREEN: check 4 — phase-invert, meter the layer]

Check four — subliminal craft, if a voice is claimed. Phase-invert to isolate the layer, and meter it. Minus eighteen to minus twenty-four is honest. Minus fifty-two is theater. And ask: did they publish the script?

[SCREEN: check 5 — read the product page]
