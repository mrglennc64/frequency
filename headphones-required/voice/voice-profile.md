# Glenn Carter — Voice Profile
*Built from `glenn-writing-samples.md`, Jul 3, 2026. This drives every voice pass on the manuscript. Read with `VOICE-GUIDE.md`.*

## The core voice
A **warm, evangelistic teacher-insider.** Glenn explains hard ideas through homely physical analogies, talks straight to "you," radiates conviction ("trust me, it works"), and positions himself as someone who knows the *real* thing that the novices and influencers don't. Underneath the enthusiasm is a craftsman who's genuinely irritated that fakers have flooded his field.

## Two gears (he writes in both)
1. **Polished** (his book prose): measured, warm, analogy-first; bolds the one key sentence in a passage; eases in with "Let's start by…", "Here's a simple way to think about it."
2. **Raw** (notes/emails): lowercase, ellipses and em-dashes everywhere, ALL-CAPS for emphasis, run-ons, numbered quick-lists, breathless momentum, likes a formula.

**For the book:** gear 1's warmth and structure, sharpened by gear 2's bluntness and conviction — but cleaned of the typos, run-ons, and lowercase.

## Signature moves — KEEP these (they're his fingerprint)
- **Everyday physical analogies** to teach: seeds in a garden, mind as a radio tuning frequencies, **two guitars resonating across a room** (that's literally entrainment — use it), magnet. This is the single most "Glenn" thing and it's perfect for explaining audio. Lean in hard.
- **Direct address + reassurance:** "try it and you'll be pleasantly surprised," "you can think it's crazy… but it's real."
- **Insider/whistleblower framing:** he frames knowledge as something most people *don't* get and he *does*.
- **One bolded key line** per passage.
- **Loves a named framework / formula and numbered steps** (the "success formula," "1. alpha 2. reprogram 3. action"). Give the book named standards and clean numbered lists — it suits him.
- **Momentum and enthusiasm;** short declaratives; the occasional exclamation.

## ⚠ The tension that needs Glenn's decision (voice vs. claims)
A large part of the raw writing asserts, *as science*, exactly the claim-types "Headphones Required" is built to scrutinize:
- Law of Attraction as physics ("like attracts like"; guitar resonance offered as *proof* of LoA);
- pineal gland → psychic ability / astral & causal planes; **fluoride calcifies the pineal**;
- chakras "constantly emitting energy… everything is energy";
- "REMEMBER THIS IS SCIENCE" applied to manifestation.

The book's entire credibility engine is the opposite discipline: separate what's *measurable* (carriers, beats, ramps, levels) from mechanistic claims that can't be shown, and it already debunks "528 Hz repairs DNA" — which is the same shape of claim as pineal/fluoride/LoA-as-physics. So:

> **Take his VOICE completely — cadence, warmth, analogies, insider-authority, momentum. But the CLAIMS have to stay inside the book's evidence-first stance, or the whistleblower position collapses (the same landmine as the Rife decision).**
