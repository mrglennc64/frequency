## Outline

### Part I — The Problem (the hook)
1. **The Bank-Account Gurus** — how manifestation audio became a get-rich-quick genre; why the people showing you their income never show you their process; the AI-sludge flood of 2023–2026.
2. **The Test Almost Everything Fails** — a simple experiment the reader can run in 10 minutes on any "binaural" track (mono-sum test); why most of what they've been listening to physically cannot work.
3. **Why This Matters** — placebo isn't harmless when people are paying for it and blaming themselves when "manifestation fails."

### Part II — How It Actually Works (the education)
4. **What a Binaural Beat Really Is** — two carriers, two ears, one brain; why the beat exists only in your head; why headphones are not a suggestion. (Title chapter.)
5. **The Brainwave Bands, Honestly** — delta/theta/alpha/beta/gamma; what entrainment research actually supports vs. what marketing claims; how to read the claims critically.
6. **Isochronic, Monaural, Binaural, "8D"** — what each actually is, when each is appropriate, and why "8D binaural" is a contradiction in terms.
7. **The Frequency Myths** — 528 Hz "DNA repair," 432 Hz conspiracy, Solfeggio origins; what's historical, what's invented, what's harmless preference vs. false claim.

### Part III — The Craft (the 30 years)
8. **Anatomy of a Correct Session** — carriers, beat, ramp-in/plateau/ramp-out, loudness targets, session length by intent. Annotated construction diagrams.
9. **Subliminals Done Right** — masking thresholds, consistent offsets, why −52 dB buried-beyond-perception layers are theater; the ethics of subliminal content.
10. **Listening Practice** — headphone selection (no, they don't need to be expensive), volume, environment, timing, consistency; why the practice around the audio matters more than the audio.
11. **How to Audit Any Track Yourself** — the full checklist: mono-sum, spectral peek with free tools, stereo-field check, ramp inspection. (This chapter quietly sells the professional audit for those who'd rather not DIY.)

### Part IV — The Practice (the results question)
12. **What the Audio Can and Can't Do** — entrainment as a state tool, not a wish engine; attention, sleep onset, relaxation — the defensible territory.
13. **A 30-Day Protocol** — an honest, structured practice combining sessions with the behavioral work the gurus skip; measurement journal included.
14. **Building or Buying** — how to evaluate any creator or tool going forward; the construction-certificate standard; what to demand before you pay anyone (including me).

### Appendices
- A: The Verified Session standard (certificate spec — seeds FrequencyForge)
- B: Free analysis tools and how to use them
- C: Research reading list — actual studies, honestly summarized including limitations
