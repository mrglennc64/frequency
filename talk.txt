Let’s start by understanding that our minds have a natural ability to attract things that match the energy of our thoughts. If we think positive, uplifting thoughts, we’re like a magnet for positive experiences and people. But if we’re often thinking negative or fearful thoughts, we’re more likely to attract situations that match those low-energy feelings. This is called the **Law of Attraction**—like attracts like. Imagine your mind as a radio station: the “frequency” of your thoughts tunes you into similar frequencies in life.


The energy we send out through our thoughts is what I call **thought power**. It’s the idea that your mind doesn’t just observe life; it helps create it. When you really understand this, you’ll see how focusing on good, positive thoughts can be a huge step toward making positive changes in your life. And the wonderful thing is, we can train our minds to grow more and more of these “good seeds.”


Here’s a simple way to think about it: **Positive thoughts create a positive energy flow. Negative thoughts create a negative flow.** By planting more positive seeds, you set yourself up to experience more joy, opportunity, and peace.
